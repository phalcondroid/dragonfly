## 0.0.1

* Initial release — annotation classes for the Dragonfly framework.
* Includes annotations for models (`@FactoryModel`, `@Field`, `@Aggregate`, `@ValueObject`, `@DomainEvent`),
  state management (`@StateModel`, `@StateManager`, `@Event`, `@StateView`),
  networking (`@Repository`, `@Get`, `@Post`, `@Put`, `@Patch`, `@Delete`, `@Path`, `@Query`, `@Body`, `@Header`,
  `@Subscribe`, `@Authenticated`),
  dependency injection (`@UseCase`, `@InjectableInit`, `@Injectable`, `@Singleton`, `@LazySingleton`, `@Inject`),
  routing (`@RouterConfig`, `@Screen`, `@PathParam`, `@QueryParam`),
  forms (`@FormSchema`, `@Required`, `@Email`, `@MinLength`, `@MaxLength`, and 20+ other validators),
  and session management (`@SessionConfig`).
